Our Services

Website Development

Standard Tier

New businesses needing a professional digital storefront to establish credibility and capture local search traffic.

  • 3-to-5 page informational website (Home, About, Services, Contact).
  • Mobile-responsive design
  • Basic SEO setup
  • Standard contact form
  • Google Business Profile connection
Intermediate Tier

Growing businesses that need to process online appointments, sell a small inventory, or track visitor behavior.

  • 5 to 10-page CMS website (e.g., WordPress)
  • Includes integration for a basic booking system or simple e-commerce (up to 50 products)
  • Blog setup
  • Google Analytics tracking
Premium Tier

Established, high-volume businesses requiring custom workflows, deep integrations, and large-scale digital operations.

  • Fully custom-developed web application
  • Includes advanced e-commerce (unlimited products, complex variations)
  • Secure user login portals
  • Custom API integrations with accounting/inventory software
  • Advanced speed optimization

Mobile Apps

Standard Tier

Businesses wanting an app-like presence on a customer's phone without the high cost of native app store development.

  • Progressive Web App (PWA)
  • A highly optimized mobile version of the website that can be saved to a phone's home screen
  • Basic offline capabilities
  • Simple push notifications
Intermediate Tier
Retailers, car washes, or restaurants focused on building repeat customer loyalty and direct mobile engagement.
  • Cross-platform mobile app (available on iOS and Android)
  • User accounts
  • Digital loyalty point systems
  • In-App messaging
  • Direct mobile ordering or service booking
Premium Tier
High-scale franchises or tech-centric startups requiring heavy processing power, hardware integration, or complex user journeys.
  • Fully custom native mobile application
  • Complex backend databases
  • Real-time geolocation tracking (e.g., for deliveries)
  • Secure in-app payment gateways
  • Advanced user analytics

AI Integration

Standard Tier

Businesses that want to reduce the volume of basic repetitive questions filtering through their phone lines.

  • Basic rule-based website chatbot
  • Programmed with static responses to frequently asked questions, trading hours, and location details using simple button-click menus
Intermediate Tier

High-traffic shops (like phone repair or salons) needing intelligent automated customer service that understands natural language.

  • LLM-powered conversational chatbot trained on specific business data (PDF price lists, service details)
  • WhatsApp integration
  • Intelligent lead capture to collect customer contact info before handing off to a human
Premium Tier

Operations seeking to fully automate their customer intake, support, and administrative booking processes 24/7.

  • Full AI workflow automation
  • Includes multi-channel conversational agents (Website, WhatsApp, Social Media) capable of dynamic appointment scheduling directly into your calendar
  • Automated quote generation
  • CRM syncing
